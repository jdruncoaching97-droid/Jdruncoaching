JD Run Coaching

Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

JD Run Coaching is run by Joe, a musculoskeletal physiotherapist. This page explains what information I collect, why, and who can see it.

What I collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email, goal, running background and the option you're interested in.
  • Athlete accounts: your name, email and password. Passwords are handled by the login service and I can't see them.
  • Training information: the runs you log (distance, time, effort, notes), any niggle or pain scores and notes you enter, and the training plan I write for you. This can include health information such as injuries.

Why I collect it

To reply to your enquiry, set up your coaching, write and adjust your training, and keep you training safely. I don't sell your information and I don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your training information is visible to you and to me. Other athletes can't see it. Your enquiry is visible only to me.

Where it is stored

This site uses a few services to run:

  • Supabase stores accounts, training plans and logs, and enquiries.
  • Netlify hosts the website. If saving an enquiry to the database fails, the form is sent to Netlify instead, so it isn't lost.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

These providers may store information on servers outside Australia. This site does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, including your account and logged runs. Use the enquiry form on the home page and I'll reply by email. If you stop coaching, tell me and I'll remove your account on request.

Questions or complaints

Contact me first through the enquiry form. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If I change this page, I'll update the date at the top.
